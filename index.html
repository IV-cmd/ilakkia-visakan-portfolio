About

I work on the layers between applications and hardware: Kubernetes, Linux, CI/CD, observability, and database reliability. My degree is in civil engineering; the through-line is systems thinking — constraints, failure modes, and operations under load.

I prefer small, inspectable systems and honest measurements over broad claims. Most of my public work is in distributed storage, recovery automation, monitoring, and publishing tooling.
